Karanphool Jadau — 22ct

The stones are chosen first

Uncut polki is bought by weight in Jaipur and sorted for a single earring over several sittings. Only stones that match in tone and depth go forward.

The gold is laid before the stone

Pure 24-carat kundan foil is worked cold around each stone, layer over layer, until the setting holds without heat or claw.

Nine weeks at the karkhana

Meenakari on the reverse alone takes eleven days. The piece is stamped, numbered and entered in the family bahi.

Craftsmanship

Naksha

Every commission is drawn at full size on tracing paper before any metal is cut. Nothing is modelled on a screen.

Dhalai

Gold is melted and alloyed to the shop's own formula, then rolled, annealed and beaten over three to five days.

Jadai

One jadiya carries a piece from the first stone to the last, seating each by hand under a loupe.

Ghisai

Eleven days of polishing on wheels of decreasing grade, finished with a cloth and rouge worked by hand.
